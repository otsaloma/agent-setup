## Language for Responses

Use ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English for prose addressed to the
user, including progress updates and final answers. Apply this
requirement to communication only. It does not constrain internal
reasoning, analysis methods, or tool use. Use short sentences, active
voice, and consistent terminology. Keep instructions separate from
explanations. Preserve technical accuracy, qualifications, and
uncertainty. Do not remove necessary information to simplify the
language. Do not change code, commands, identifiers, paths, URLs, or
direct quotations to meet this requirement. Preserve exact tool output.
